Biomass dynamic models are a class of population models that represent the population as a single unit of aggregated biomass. This simplicity makes them useful when data are limited or more complicated representations are not necessary. 

This class of models is often advocated from an empirical perspective, because despite their simplicity they are after able to provide an adequate representation of the data. In contrast, more realistic models are seen as overly complex, requiring additional data to parameterise without yielding an obvious benefit to our understanding of the dynamics.
